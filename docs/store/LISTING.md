## Full description (max 4000)

ABC Kids helps toddlers and preschoolers learn the alphabet, letter sounds and first words through short, gentle lessons and simple games.

LEARN ALL 26 LETTERS
• A to Z letter book with big capital and small letters
• A friendly picture for every letter: apple, ball, cat, duck, egg and more
• Swipe or tap the big arrows to go through the whole alphabet

PHONICS LESSONS, ONE SOUND AT A TIME
• Follows a common phonics order: s, a, t, p, i, n, m, d, g, o and on to z
• Each lesson: meet the sound, discover picture words, play two short games, earn a sticker
• Words that start with each sound, read aloud with your device voice
• Grown-up prompts help you say each sound together with your child

FUN LEARNING GAMES
• Feed the Monster: choose the picture that starts with the sound
• Sound Safari: hear a word and find its picture
• Pop the Letter: pop the bubble with the matching letter
• Letter Parking: park each car in the garage with the same letter
• Build a Letter: put the pieces together to make the letter shape
• Magic Trail: optional letter tracing, following the arrows

NUMBERS AND SHAPES
• Explore numbers 1 to 10 and count objects
• Find and match circles, squares, triangles and rectangles

MADE FOR LITTLE HANDS
• Big buttons, one simple Play button, very short instructions
• Gentle help after a wrong answer: listen again, fewer choices, then a demonstration
• Stickers to celebrate every lesson and game
• Optional break reminders at a natural pause

FOR PARENTS
• Parent area behind a grown-ups check
• See which sounds your child has explored and practised
• Choose guided or free play, readiness level, tracing, letter case and sound order
• Several child profiles on one device: nickname and avatar only, no accounts or sign-up
• Sound, music, larger text, reduce motion and caption settings

FAMILY-FRIENDLY CONTENT
Pictures and words are chosen for young children and families of all backgrounds, with no pork products, alcohol, gambling, violence or rude words.

ADS
There are no ads while your child plays. A single banner is shown only inside the parent area.
