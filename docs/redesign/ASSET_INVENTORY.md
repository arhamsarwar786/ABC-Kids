## Words per sound (lesson content)

s: sun, sock, snake · a: ant, apple, alligator · t: tiger, tomato, tent ·
p: pear, pizza (vegetable toppings), penguin · i: insect, ink, igloo · n: nose, nut, net · m: moon, mouse, monkey ·
b: ball, banana, bus · c: cat, cup · d: duck, door · e: egg, envelope · f: fish, flower · g: goat, grapes ·
h: hat, house · j: jug, jam · k: kite, key · l: lion, leaf · o: octopus, olive · q: quilt, queen ·
r: rainbow, rocket · u: umbrella, up · v: van, vase · w: whale, watermelon · y: yo-yo, yoghurt · z: zebra, zip ·
x: box, fox (**end-sound pictures**, `ContentCatalog.endSoundWords`)

Each lesson word records its beginning sound; a test asserts it matches the
letter’s taught sound. x has no beginning-sound words, so its lesson shows box
and fox with “x is at the end” and plans no Feed the Monster or Sound Safari.
Sounds that start alike (c, k: /k/; q: /kw/) are never distractors for each
other, and mirror-image letters (b/d, p/q, b/p, d/q, n/u, m/w) are never put
side by side as choices.

Stroke shapes exist for all 26 lowercase letters (`letter_strokes.dart`, still
**unverified**: a teacher should check formation before release).

## Content suitability

Content is chosen to suit Muslim families and toddlers: no pig or pork
products, no alcohol, gambling, violence or rude words. `test/content_test.dart`
fails if any blocked word appears in picture words, picture descriptions or any
string literal in `lib/`. The original “pig” word was replaced by “pear”, and
the pizza illustration uses vegetable toppings.
